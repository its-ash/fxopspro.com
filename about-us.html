What We Do

Core services built for emerging brokers and proprietary trading firms.

Brokerage Backend Operations

We manage and support critical backend functions for up-and-coming brokers, helping them establish stable and efficient operational frameworks. From technical setup to ongoing operational support, our goal is to simplify the complexities of running a brokerage.

Liquidity Provider Consulting

Choosing the right liquidity partner is crucial for any trading business. FxOpsPro assists brokers in evaluating and selecting suitable liquidity providers (LPs), ensuring better spreads, execution quality, and reliable market access. We also advise on liquidity configurations and pricing structures to optimize trading conditions.

Technology & Infrastructure Solutions

The trading industry relies heavily on robust technology. We help brokers select and integrate essential technology solutions including trading servers, bridges, CRM systems, and risk management tools that align with their business model.
