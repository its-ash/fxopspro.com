What Is Prop Trading?

Prop Trading is a business where traders join a trading contest using virtual money. The goal is to follow the contest rules and perform well with the provided accounts.

Top traders who succeed get to manage a real trading account as a reward and earn based on a reward system agreed upon with the prop firm.

Why it matters

  • High-demand challenge model attracts global traders.
  • Clear rules and rewards improve trust and retention.
  • Path to live accounts turns performance into revenue.
